为什么“全球进口复杂度排名”不能直接拿来决策

一份排名如果没有公开的逐国数据、计算过程和可复算的赋分，就不是可验证的基准。用户提供的 PDF 自建了一个 100 分指标，把税费结构、许可审批、政策变化和查验摩擦合成一个总分，但没有给出逐国分项证据与完整参考文献。因此，本文不把其中的分数和名次当作事实发布。

这份 PDF 可以作为问题清单的草稿，不能作为国家排名的证据。关税税率、进口增值税、退税、许可要求、查验概率和港口效率是不同变量；适用范围也会随商品编码、原产地、贸易协定和进口商身份改变。把它们压成一个总分，会制造虚假的精确感。

公开指标也有边界。世界银行的物流绩效指标衡量物流体系表现；OECD 的贸易便利化指标关注透明度、手续简化、自动化和边境协作。它们适合观察制度和物流环境，却不替企业计算某个 HS 编码的税费、强制许可、资金占用和 ERP 入账成本。

English: A ranking without country-level evidence, transparent calculations and reproducible scoring is not a decision-grade benchmark. The supplied PDF proposes a 100-point index but does not provide verifiable country-by-country score evidence or a complete reference list. Its scores should not be presented as established facts.

从国家总分切换到“商品 × 业务流程”评估。每项都要记录主管机构、适用商品、数据日期、金额口径和待确认事项。

归类与准入

02

到岸成本

03

许可与标准

04

申报与放行

明确申报系统、所需单证、查验安排和异常补料路径。

05

发票与入账

把税务凭证、成本分摊、应付账款和 ERP 记录连接起来。

执行顺序：先用商品编码和原产地锁定监管规则；再测税费和资金需求；再确认许可证与申报路径；最后验证单证、发票及财务数据是否能贯通。比较市场时，用同一商品、进口主体、贸易术语和成本口径进行横向对照。

English: Assess five things for each product-market route: classification and eligibility; landed taxes and working capital; permits and technical standards; declaration and release; and invoice-to-ledger integration. Use the same product, importer, Incoterm and cost basis when comparing markets.

结论：别问哪个国家最复杂，问这票货怎样落地

统一口径国家比较必须固定商品、原产地、进口主体、贸易术语和成本口径。
从官方规则开始先核对税务、许可和申报要求，再评估清关效率与服务能力。
把流程接起来让单证、税费、电子发票和财务记录使用一致的数据与状态。

对企业来说，复杂度不是一张榜单上的数字，而是每次进口中能否提前发现成本、材料和审批节点。把一票业务拆清楚，比记住一个没有依据的国家名次更有用。

English: Do not ask which country is “most complex” in the abstract. Ask what it takes to land this product, for this importer, under this trade arrangement. A documented route beats an unsupported league table.

Global Import Complexity: Stop Chasing a Single Ranking

Start with the landed cost and operating path for a specific product, importer and trade arrangement.

A country-wide score cannot tell a company whether a particular product needs a licence, how much cash will be tied up, which declaration route applies or whether invoice data will reach the ledger. The useful unit of analysis is a route: country × product × importer × trade terms.

The supplied PDF creates a 100-point index covering taxes, permits, policy volatility and border friction. It does not include a reproducible country-by-country scoring file or a complete bibliography. We therefore do not repeat its scores as verified facts.

Use five checks instead

01

Classification

HS code, origin rules, restrictions and product eligibility.

02

Landed cost

Duty, import taxes, local charges, service fees and working capital.

03

Licences

Permits, technical standards, inspections and agency approvals.

04

Release path

Declaration system, documents, inspection and exception handling.

05

Invoice to ledger

Fiscal documents, cost allocation, payables and ERP records.

Brazil: identify the route before configuring the workflow

Brazil continues a phased transition between legacy DI/LI procedures and Duimp/LPCO. The applicable path depends on the product’s administrative treatment and rollout status. NF-e is a fiscal e-invoice, not the customs declaration. Check the current official requirements for each shipment.
