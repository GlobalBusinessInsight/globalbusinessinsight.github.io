# Search growth operating plan

This site is organized around a small number of useful topic hubs rather than a large set of thin keyword pages. Every new article should answer one reader question, state its date and geography, link to its primary sources, and connect to a relevant hub and two related reports.

## Priority keyword groups

| Search intent | Chinese examples | English examples | Landing page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Supply chain research | 美国第三方仓储、3PL 仓储、仓储成本 | U.S. 3PL warehousing, warehouse fulfillment cost | `/topics/warehousing/` |
| Going global | 工程机械出海、南美市场、海外渠道 | construction equipment overseas, South America market entry | `/topics/global-machinery/` |
| Enterprise technology | 企业 AI、SAP S/4HANA、制造业数字化 | enterprise AI, SAP S/4HANA manufacturing | `/topics/enterprise-ai/` |
| Market context | 美联储利率、美国国债、美国宏观数据 | U.S. rates, Treasury yields, U.S. macro data | `/markets/` |

## Publishing rules

1. Put the main reader question in the title and the first paragraph. Use a specific market, period, and business context where possible.
2. Write a distinct title and description for each page. Avoid repeating a generic “深度分析” title across unrelated topics.
3. Add descriptive internal links from the article to its topic hub, a related report, and the archive. Use anchor text that tells the reader what they will find.
4. Separate current facts, historical observations, estimates, and forecasts. Keep the source, observation date, unit, geography, and definition next to important numbers.
5. Update a page when its facts or sources change; do not change dates only to make an old page appear new.
6. After publishing, submit `/sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console and inspect the page URL. Review queries and impressions monthly, then improve pages that already receive impressions before creating new variants.

Google's guidance: [create helpful, reliable, people-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content), [write useful title links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link), and [use crawlable links with meaningful anchor text](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable).
